Median time from lead in to first dial, per client. The longer a lead waits, the worse: green under 5 min, red over 30.

How the dialer itself is performing this window.

Funnel

Cumulative by event date, not current stage.

Trend

Leads, worked and booked, with median speed to lead on the right axis.

Connect rate by number

Every number we dial from, worst first. A number that keeps dialing but stops connecting is the signature of carrier filtering.

Dials by state

Where our numbers are registered, by volume. Area code is where the number was issued, not where the lead lives.

Number health

One row per dialing number. Tap a row for which clients it is being used on and how it connects for each. Rates are withheld until a number has enough dials to judge.
Client SDR conversion performanceSAMPLE
This window's share of the $0 monthly book, divided by this window's real dialer activity. The $/Dial on the Clients tab is a different cut: annual value over each client's lifetime dials.

Revenue by client

Monthly recurring revenue from closed deals. Top 12 of 0 paying clients.

Revenue per dial

Annual value returned per dial, for the clients we dial most. Higher is better: green at $600+, red under $200.

Setters

Sortable on every column. Attempts and dials are separate on purpose: the ratio is the attempt definition working. Default sort: book rate.

Coverage

Day-one coverage and form compliance per setter, against target. These two predict the rest of the table.

Clients

One row per client, sorted by MRR. Leads, dials and dollar figures are lifetime totals; Status is book rate for the selected period against the 15% target. Tap a row for its funnel, stage lags and SDR economics.
